# Metrics

The most important metric is completed audits per visitor. If people do not finish the audit, the savings estimate never gets a chance to create value.

Secondary metrics:

- Form start rate: whether the homepage makes the value clear.
- Tool rows per audit: whether the app captures meaningful stacks or toy inputs.
- Average estimated savings: whether the rules are finding material issues.
- Email capture rate after results: whether users trust the report enough to keep it.
- Share click rate: whether the report is useful for internal discussion.

I would avoid optimizing for raw signups early. SpendLens earns trust by showing useful findings before asking for anything.
