# Go-To-Market

Primary channel: founder and engineering communities where AI tool spend is visible pain.

1. Search X for founders mentioning Cursor, Claude Team, Copilot Business, or "AI tool bill" and offer a free 2-minute audit.
2. Post a teardown in Indie Hackers and relevant Slack/Discord founder communities: "I audited a fake 12-person startup's AI bill and found $340/month in waste."
3. Create comparison pages for "Cursor vs Copilot cost", "Claude Team 5 seat minimum", and "AI tool budget for startups".

The first conversion metric is completed audits, not email captures. Email capture is secondary because the product needs trust before it asks for a relationship.
